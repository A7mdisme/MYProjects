Greenhouse Temperature Detector

This Tinkercad project uses an Arduino Uno and a TMP36 temperature sensor to monitor a simulated greenhouse. A red LED provides a visible heat warning, and a vibration motor provides a second alert. The Serial Monitor displays the temperature in degrees Celsius.

How it works
The Arduino reads the sensor on pin A0 and converts the reading to degrees Celsius. When the measured temperature reaches 31°C or higher, the Arduino turns on the LED through pin 7 and signals a transistor through pin 4 to switch on the motor. Both alerts turn off when the temperature drops to 30°C or below. Between 30°C and 31°C, they keep their previous state so small temperature changes do not repeatedly switch them on and off.

How to test it
Start the simulation, open the Serial Monitor, and select the TMP36. Set the temperature to 25°C, raise it to 35°C, then lower it to 25°C again. The LED and motor should turn on at the higher temperature and turn off as the greenhouse cools.
